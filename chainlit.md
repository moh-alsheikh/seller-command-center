# Seller Command Center

Your chief of staff for the shop. Ask a business question and it puts the right specialists
on it, then gives you a short brief with recommended next steps.

| Specialist | Ask about |
|---|---|
| **Sales analyst** | revenue, orders, trends, why a number changed |
| **Customer insights** | best customers, who is at risk, repeat purchases |
| **Inventory planner** | what to restock and roughly how many units |
| **Pricing strategist** | price tiers, prices in other currencies |
| **Listing writer** | product listings from real sales facts |

**Good to know**

- "Today" is the **last day in the shop's data**, the most recent Friday when the data was
  built. The current month is a partial month.
- All money is in **GBP**. The data has no stock levels or costs.
- Actions such as **draft purchase orders** and **listing drafts** only happen after you click
  **Approve**.
- Tell it your preferences ("focus on Germany", "our target margin is 40%") and it remembers
  them across chats.
- Expand a step ("Used Inventory planner · 4.1s") to see exactly which tools ran and what they
  returned.

Data: UCI Online Retail II (CC BY 4.0): real transactions, with dates moved forward to the
present for this demo (weekdays and all figures unchanged).
